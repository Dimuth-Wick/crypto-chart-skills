# Evidence grades

Use these grades on the chart (`"grade"` in the spec). **Never grade higher.**
Full sources: `docs/research.md` in the repository.

| Grade key | Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `moderate` | 🟢 MODERATE | Some academic support in crypto or strong support in other markets; still weakened by fees and regime changes |
| `weak` | 🟡 WEAK | Mixed or limited evidence; context only |
| `unproven` | ⚪ UNPROVEN | Popular, but no rigorous study found |
| `no_edge` | 🔴 NO EDGE | Studies found little or no predictive value |

## Signals

| Signal | Grade | Why (short) |
|---|---|---|
| Market structure: HH-HL uptrend, LH-LL downtrend | `moderate` | Trend / range-breakout rules showed excess returns in crypto studies; image-based trend signals predictive in equities (Jiang, Kelly & Xiu 2023). Costs and bubbles reduce gains |
| Break of structure (close beyond last swing) | `moderate` | Same trading-range breakout evidence |
| Range breakout (close outside a horizontal range) | `moderate` | Trading-range breakout studies |
| Triangle **breakout** (close outside the triangle) | `moderate` | Graded as a range/structure breakout. The shape itself has no dedicated crypto study |
| Triangle **before** breakout | `unproven` | No evidence that the shape predicts direction |
| Head & shoulders / inverse | `weak` | Mixed: some information in US stocks (Lo, Mamaysky & Wang 2000; Savin et al.), unprofitable on average per Osler. No strong crypto study |
| Double top / double bottom | `weak` | Some information in equities; early-Bitcoin evidence may not apply today |
| Support / resistance breakouts | `weak` | Widely used, little rigorous crypto evidence. Use for stops and targets |
| Flags, pennants, wedges | `unproven` | No rigorous crypto study found |
| Candlestick patterns (hammer, engulfing, doji, ...) | `no_edge` | 68 patterns on top-23 coins of little use (IEEE 2021); 8 of 41 showed potential in a later study, 2 worked opposite to textbook |

## How to talk about grades

- MODERATE does not mean "likely to win". It means "better supported than the rest".
- Combining weak signals does not make a strong one.
- If no pattern is clear, show structure only. That is a complete answer.
